Soyla

Push-to-talk dictation for macOS that keeps Uzbek, Russian and English in one sentence.

Download View on GitHub

Free and open source · macOS 14+ · Apple silicon

Why Soyla

  • Code-switching is the point

    Most dictation apps pick one language per recording. Soyla sends your audio to a general-purpose model with a prompt built for mixed Uzbek, Russian and English speech. On a 41-second clip that switches languages mid-sentence, the default setup kept both scripts in 7 of 7 runs.

  • Your key, your data

    There is no Soyla account and no Soyla server. Audio goes from your Mac straight to the provider you choose, with your own API key. Nothing is sent to the author.

  • Built and measured in the open

    Every dictation is logged locally with a full latency timeline. The replay tool re-sends the same recording to other models, so comparisons use identical audio.

How it works

  1. Hold fn

    Click into any text field and hold the key.

  2. Speak

    Talk the way you actually talk. Uzbek in Latin, Russian in Cyrillic, no language switch to flip.

  3. Release

    The text lands where your cursor is. Median 2.3 s from release to pasted text.

Privacy

  • No account. Nothing to sign up for.
  • No Soyla server. Audio goes from your Mac to the provider you choose.
  • Your own key. OpenRouter or Google AI Studio, saved in your Mac's Keychain.
  • No telemetry. Soyla never looks at your screen.

Inside the app

Settings are pages of plain rows, each with one line on what it does.

Known limits

  • Not signed by Apple yet, so the first open of each version takes the Open Anyway detour.
  • Online only. No model runs on the Mac; offline dictations are kept and retried later.
  • fn is an Apple-keyboard key. On other keyboards pick another hotkey in Settings.
  • Gemini free tier trains on your audio. Use OpenRouter or a paid key for anything confidential.
